# Open World Space Tycoon

Open World Space Tycoon is a Phaser 2D browser game about flying a ship through a small open galaxy, trading goods, managing fuel and ammo, and moving between connected maps through wormholes.

## What is in the game

- Open-space flight with gravity around suns and planets
- Stations with trading, fuel, ammo, and shipyard services
- Fuel and ammo management
- Multiple maps connected through gates
- A simple market economy with different station pricing
- Persistent player save data

## Controls

- `Arrow Keys`: Fly and rotate
- `Space`: Fire
- `E`: Open the station market
- `R`: Close the station market
- `G`: Jump through a gate
- `N`: Start a new game
